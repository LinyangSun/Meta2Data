Meta2Data reference classifier notices
=====================================

These notices accompany Meta2Data-maintained classifier builds for
Greengenes2 2024.09 and SILVA 138.1. They identify the source databases,
their redistribution terms, and the changes specified by the build recipe.

Applicable resource filenames:
  gg2-2024.09-full-length-sklearn-1.7.1.qza
  silva-138.1-full-length-sklearn-1.7.1.qza

The builds are maintained by the Meta2Data project. Database attribution does
not imply endorsement by Greengenes2, SILVA, or their contributors.

Official release: https://ftp.microbio.me/greengenes_release/2024.09/
Official license: https://ftp.microbio.me/greengenes_release/2024.09/00LICENSE

  https://ftp.microbio.me/greengenes_release/2024.09/00README

  https://ftp.microbio.me/greengenes_release/2024.09/2024.09.backbone.full-length.fna.qza
  https://ftp.microbio.me/greengenes_release/2024.09/2024.09.backbone.tax.qza

Maintainer build: the official full-length backbone sequences and their
matching taxonomy are used to retrain a naive Bayes classifier in the
Meta2Data QIIME 2 2026.7 / scikit-learn 1.7.1 environment. The database version
and source taxonomy are retained. Exact training parameters and source
identities are specified in scripts/build_reference_classifiers.py and are
recorded in the model build manifest.

Changes specified by the Meta2Data maintainer build recipe: SILVA 138.1
SSURef NR99 sequences and taxonomy files are imported into QIIME 2; taxonomy
is parsed into fixed ranks from domain to genus with rank propagation and
without species labels; RNA sequences are converted to DNA; sequences are
filtered for degenerate bases, homopolymers, and taxon-specific minimum
lengths; sequences and taxonomy are dereplicated; a full-length uniform
naive Bayes classifier is trained in the Meta2Data QIIME 2 2026.7 /
scikit-learn 1.7.1 environment. No primer-specific region is extracted.
Exact sources and parameters are specified in
scripts/build_reference_classifiers.py and are recorded in the model build
manifest. This describes the preparation recipe, not a new SILVA release.
